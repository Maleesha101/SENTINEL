# Player Guide

## Mission
You are investigating unusual activity in SENTINEL, an internal operations platform used to coordinate autonomous pharmaceutical delivery vehicles.

Your objective is to understand the application's workflows and identify a path to the protected challenge objective.

## Rules
- Stay inside the provided lab environment.
- Do not target external systems.
- Do not rely on denial-of-service techniques.
- Use the application as an application-security testing target.

## Suggested methodology
- Map the application roles and workflows.
- Inspect how incident reports are created and reviewed.
- Observe browser/API behavior.
- Compare what different identifiers return.
- Trace relationships between telemetry and maintenance records.
- Pay attention to server-side authorization decisions.

The full attack chain is intentionally not documented here.
